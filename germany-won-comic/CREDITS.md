# Credits

- **Map:** Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 (public domain), via `world-atlas` 2.0.2 (ISC) on the jsDelivr CDN; decoded and clipped by `tools/map.py`. Occupation is drawn as hand-cut paper patches, not as borders.
- **Fonts:** Nunito and Russo One (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- **Engine:** the Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon drawing engine from the Lemo-Opuscar library (MIT), with the «А что если — круглоголовые» look (round heads, coloured stickmen).
- **Music and sound:** synthesized in `mix.py` (numpy/scipy), no samples.
- Puppets personify sides, not real people; no symbols, flags or insignia.
